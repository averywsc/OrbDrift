Orb Drift

Orb Drift is a WebXR arena shooter you can play in a VR headset or any browser, with three hard modes, boss fights and a global leaderboard.

Desktop: just click the orbs, no looking around needed · VR: trigger to shoot, hold for rapid fire
Cyan +10 · magenta chargers +25 · gold +50 and a life or time · red bombs cost a life
